📜 Game Rules

⚔️ 1. Card Power

Empereur beats Citoyen.

Citoyen beats Esclave.

Esclave beats Empereur.

🏆 2. Scoring

The winner gets 1 point or 5 points depending on the round.

In case of a tie, both cards are removed.

👑 3. Game Progress

The game contains 12 rounds.

The camp changes after rounds 3, 6 and 9.

🎯 4. Final Result

After 12 rounds, the player with the highest score wins!
